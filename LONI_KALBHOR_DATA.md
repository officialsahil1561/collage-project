# Loni Kalbhor Civic Intelligence Platform — Data Inventory

**Region:** Loni Kalbhor, Haveli Taluka, Pune District, Maharashtra, India  
**Administrative Authority:** Loni Kalbhor Gram Panchayat & Pune Zilla Parishad (Haveli Panchayat Samiti)  

---

## 1. Demographic Overview (Census 2011)
- **Total Population:** 27,814 (Male: 14,750 | Female: 13,064)
- **Total Households:** 6,140
- **Literacy Rate:** 84.6%
- **Scheduled Caste %:** 12.4%
- **Scheduled Tribe %:** 2.1%
- **Current Population Projection:** Estimated at ~65,000 to ~72,000 residents due to rapid suburbanization along the Pune-Solapur National Highway (NH 65) and expansion of educational institutions (e.g., MIT-ADT University).

---

## 2. Water & Sanitation Infrastructure
- **Mula-Mutha River Raw Water Intake:** 12.5 MLD capacity.
- **Jal Jeevan Mission Rural Piped Scheme:** Operational supplying ~5.1 MLD.
- **Overhead Tanks (ESR/GSR):** Kadamwak Wasti booster station (3.5 MLD).

---

## 3. Roads & Transportation
- **National Highway (NH 65):** Pune-Solapur corridor (8.4 km through Loni Kalbhor).
- **Major District Road (MDR):** Loni Kalbhor - Theur Link Road (6.2 km).
- **Railway Connectivity:** Loni Kalbhor Railway Station on the Central Railway Pune-Daund suburban section.
