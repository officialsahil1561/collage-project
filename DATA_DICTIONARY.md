# JanDhan Insights — Data Dictionary & Metric Definitions

This data dictionary defines every financial category, verification status, and civic indicator used across the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) platform.

## 1. Financial Categories
- **Budget Estimates (BE)**: Proposed or approved initial financial outlay presented by the Municipal Commissioner or approved by the Standing Committee for a financial year.
- **Revised Estimates (RE)**: Mid-year revised financial projections reflecting actual trends and supplementary adjustments.
- **Actual Expenditure**: Audited, final expenditure disbursed by PMC as published in annual audited financial statements.
- **Revenue Expenditure**: Routine operational outlays including establishment, salaries, utility bills, and maintenance.
- **Capital Expenditure**: Outlays dedicated to long-term infrastructure creation, land acquisition, water treatment plants, and civic assets.

## 2. Verification Statuses
- **Verified**: Supported by direct official government budget publications, audited statements, or open data records with accessible URLs.
- **Partially Verified (Quarantined)**: Records or sub-heads undergoing internal reconciliation or quarantined to prevent double counting against consolidated totals.
- **Data Not Available**: Official figures or granular breakdowns not published in machine-readable open datasets or audit reports.

## 3. Civic Metrics
- **LPCD (Litres Per Capita Daily)**: Average water supply distribution per resident per day.
- **MLD (Million Litres per Day)**: Daily capacity or treatment volume for water and sewage treatment plants.
- **TPD (Tonnes Per Day)**: Daily tonnage of municipal solid waste processed or handled.
- **Drainage Coverage %**: Percentage of households connected to underground municipal sewerage networks within ward boundaries.
