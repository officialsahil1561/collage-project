# Loni Kalbhor — Research & Integration Methodology

1. **Jurisdictional Separation:** Loni Kalbhor is treated as an independent rural / semi-urban governing body (Gram Panchayat under Haveli Taluka and Pune Zilla Parishad) and is strictly separated from Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) financial aggregates and ward listings.
2. **Census vs. Projections:** Historical Census 2011 statistics are clearly separated from contemporary demographic estimates (~65,000–72,000).
3. **Financial Accounting:** Local Gram Panchayat receipts, house tax collections, 15th Finance Commission grants, and Jal Jeevan Mission funds are tracked independently without mixing with municipal municipal municipal budgets.
