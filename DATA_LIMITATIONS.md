# JanDhan Insights — Data Limitations & Scope Constraints

Academic and technical transparency requires clear disclosure of platform limitations:
1. **Ongoing Fiscal Years**: For FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27, final audited actual expenditures and revised estimates are pending full financial year closure and statutory audit.
2. **Granular Ward Actual Spending**: Machine-readable ward-wise actual expenditure tables are not published in open CSV formats by PMC. Consequently, ward-level actual spending displays `"Data Not Available"` in strict compliance with the zero-fabrication policy.
3. **Citizen Feedback Portal**: The feedback and complaint forms simulate local drafting, validation, and JSON/CSV export. They do not maintain a persistent server-side database or guarantee live transmission to PMC grievance servers unless using the official external PMC CARE portal link (`https://complaint.pmc.gov.in/`).
